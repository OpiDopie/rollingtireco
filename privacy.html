Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

This policy explains what Rolling Tire Co. ("we", "us") collects when you use this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request service we collect your name, mobile number, vehicle year, make and model, the problem you describe, any tire size or photo you provide, and your address or map location. We receive this by email when you submit the booking form.

Your location

If you tap "Use current location", your browser asks for permission and shares your position once. We use it only to show a pin and to send a technician to you. This website does not track your location in the background.

How we use it

We use your information to provide an estimate, contact you by text or phone, perform the service, and keep business records. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

Booking requests are delivered through Web3Forms. Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap, vehicle lists come from the U.S. NHTSA vehicle database, and fonts load from Google Fonts. Each of these receives your IP address when it loads. Those providers have their own privacy policies.

Retention

We keep service records for as long as needed for our business, taxes and warranty questions, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete your information by emailing jhd7187@gmail.com. You can also call 469-642-4864.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
