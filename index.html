About Rolling Tire Co.

Rolling Tire Co. is a locally run mobile tire service for the north Dallas suburbs. We started it for a simple reason: a flat tire should not cost you a tow truck, an afternoon at a shop or a missed day of work.

A technician drives to your home, office or roadside with the equipment to repair or replace your tire on the spot. You see an estimate before you request us, and we confirm the final price before any work begins.

Common questions

How do I know what it will cost?

The booking form shows an estimate once you choose your problem. The technician confirms the final price with you before any work starts.

Can every flat be repaired?

No. Sidewall damage and large punctures cannot be repaired safely. If your tire is not repairable, we will tell you and offer a replacement.

Do I need to be there?

Yes, someone should be with the vehicle so we can confirm the work and take payment. If that is not possible, call us and we will work out a plan.

What if I am on a busy road?

Your safety comes first. Move to a parking lot or a safe shoulder if you can. If it is not safe for us to work where you are, we may ask you to move or call a tow.

Do you work outside your normal hours?

Yes. Outside 7 AM to 7 PM we offer after-hours service for an additional fee, which is included in your estimate.

Can you supply the tires?

Yes. We can quote tires for your vehicle, or install tires you already bought.
